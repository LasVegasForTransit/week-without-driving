title: 'Pay your bus fare'
order: 2
icon: 'mdi:cash-multiple'
cardSummary: 'How much a ride costs, how to pay with or without a phone, and who pays less.'
intro: >-
  RTC runs the buses in Las Vegas. Here is what a ride costs and how to pay. You don't need a
  smartphone or a bank card.
steps:
  - heading: 'Pick a pass'
    icon: 'mdi:ticket-confirmation-outline'
    picture: 'fares'
    body:
      - >-
        These are the prices for people who live here. There are also 7-day passes for $20 and
        15-day passes for $34.
      - >-
        Riding more than once? A pass can cost less. A round trip with a change of buses is four
        rides, or $8. A 24-hour pass covers it for $5.
      - >-
        Every pass works on every route, even the Deuce on the Strip. Visitors pay more to ride the
        Deuce.
  - heading: 'Pick how to pay'
    icon: 'mdi:credit-card-wireless-outline'
    body:
      - >-
        No phone or bank card? Pay with exact change when you get on. Or buy a pass with cash at a
        ticket machine or an RTC sales office. A Tap & GO card you can reload works too.
      - >-
        Have a phone? The rideRTC app sells passes. Scan your pass when you get on. You can add cash
        to your app at some local stores.
      - >-
        For one ride, you can also tap a Visa or Mastercard, phone, or watch when you get on. Have
        your fare ready before the bus comes.
  - heading: 'See if you can pay less'
    icon: 'mdi:account-heart-outline'
    body:
      - >-
        Some riders pay less: youth and students, adults 60 and older, veterans and people in the
        military, people with disabilities, Medicare card holders, and riders with mobility
        training.
      - >-
        Each group shows different proof. Apply before the day you want to ride, because it can take
        time.
      - >-
        Use a wheelchair, walker or other mobility aid? "Ride with a wheelchair, walker or stroller"
        also explains RTC Paratransit.
    note: 'Fares and rules can change. Check RTC before you go.'
    links:
      - label: 'Check current fares at rtcsnv.com'
        href: 'https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/fares-passes/'
      - label: 'Check if you can pay less'
        href: 'https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/fares-passes/reduced-fare/'
---
