---
title: 'Do a sidewalk audit'
order: 5
icon: 'mdi:clipboard-text-outline'
cardSummary: 'Note what makes a street hard to walk or roll, and tell someone who can fix it.'
intro: >-
  A sidewalk audit is a short trip where you write down what makes a street easy or hard to get
  along. You can walk, use a wheelchair, walker or cane, or push a stroller. It turns "this street
  is hard to get around" into problems someone can fix. Anyone can do one, and you don't need any
  training.
steps:
  - heading: 'Pick a short stretch you know'
    icon: 'mdi:map-marker-path'
    body:
      - >-
        Pick about 15 minutes of a trip you make often, such as the way from home to your nearest
        bus stop. Do it in daylight, however you usually get along it.
      - >-
        Go alone or bring a friend. Can't get out? You can still write down what you already know
        about a route you use.
  - heading: 'Note each problem and where it is'
    icon: 'mdi:alert-circle-outline'
    body:
      - >-
        Record each problem however works for you: a photo, a voice memo, or a written note on paper
        or your phone. For each one, note where it is (the nearest address or the cross streets) and
        what's wrong.
      - >-
        Look for a missing or broken sidewalk; something blocking the path; a corner with no curb
        ramp, or a ramp that's too steep or broken; a corner with no bumpy warning strip for people
        who are blind or have low vision; a crossing signal with no sound, or too little time to
        cross; no shade; and a bus stop with no bench, no shelter, or no room to wait with a
        wheelchair or stroller.
      - >-
        Photograph the problem, not people, and stay on the sidewalk or curb. Never stand in the
        road.
  - heading: 'Share what you found'
    icon: 'mdi:email-outline'
    body:
      - >-
        Email your photos, voice memos or notes to
        [wwd@lasvegasfortransit.org](mailto:wwd@lasvegasfortransit.org). If you use Instagram, you
        can also post them and tag [@lasvegasfortransit](https://instagram.com/lasvegasfortransit).
      - >-
        Las Vegans for Better Transit collects every report and passes it to the city, the county or
        RTC, whichever is responsible for that street or stop.
---
