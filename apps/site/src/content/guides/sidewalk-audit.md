---
title: 'Do a sidewalk audit'
order: 6
icon: 'mdi:clipboard-text-outline'
cardSummary: 'Write down what makes a street hard to walk or roll. Tell someone who can fix it.'
intro: >-
  A sidewalk audit is a short trip where you write down what makes a street hard to get along. Walk,
  roll, use a cane, or push a stroller. Anyone can do one. You don't need training.
steps:
  - heading: 'Pick a short stretch you know'
    icon: 'mdi:map-marker-path'
    body:
      - >-
        Pick about 15 minutes of a trip you make often, like home to your bus stop. Go in daylight,
        the way you usually travel.
      - >-
        Go alone or bring a friend. Can't get out? Write down what you already know about a route
        you use.
  - heading: 'Note each problem and where it is'
    icon: 'mdi:alert-circle-outline'
    picture: 'audit-list'
    body:
      - >-
        Take a photo, record a voice memo, or write a note. For each problem, say where it is (an
        address or cross streets) and what's wrong.
      - >-
        Look for the problems in the pictures above. A ramp can also be too steep or broken. A
        crossing signal can have no sound, or too little time to cross. The bumpy strip at a corner
        helps people who are blind or have low vision.
      - >-
        Take photos of the problem, not of people. Stay on the sidewalk or curb. Never stand in the
        road.
  - heading: 'Share what you found'
    icon: 'mdi:email-outline'
    body:
      - >-
        Email your photos, voice memos or notes to
        [wwd@lasvegasfortransit.org](mailto:wwd@lasvegasfortransit.org). On Instagram? Post them and
        tag [@lasvegasfortransit](https://instagram.com/lasvegasfortransit).
      - >-
        Las Vegans for Better Transit uses what you find to push for safer streets and bus stops.
---
