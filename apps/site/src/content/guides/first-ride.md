---
title: 'Ride the bus for the first time'
order: 1
icon: 'mdi:bus-stop'
cardSummary: 'Find your bus, get on, and get off at your stop. Three steps.'
intro: >-
  New to the bus? This guide walks you through one trip. RTC runs the buses in Las Vegas.
steps:
  - heading: 'Find your bus'
    icon: 'mdi:map-search-outline'
    picture: 'ride-plan'
    body:
      - >-
        Type where you're going on [Plan a trip](/go). Your map app shows the bus number, the stop,
        and when to be there.
      - >-
        The free Transit app shows where your bus is right now. So does RTC's rideRTC app.
      - >-
        Look at the time of the next bus too. Then missing one is no big deal.
  - heading: 'Get on the bus'
    icon: 'mdi:bus-side'
    picture: 'ride-board'
    body:
      - >-
        Buses stop only at bus stops. Check the sign on the front of the bus. It shows the route
        number and where the bus is going.
      - >-
        Stand where the driver can see you. Let people get off first. Then get on at the front door.
      - >-
        Pay as you get on: $2 for one ride, or $5 for 24 hours of rides. Drivers don't have change.
        [Pay your bus fare](/guides/pay-your-fare) shows every way to pay.
      - >-
        Hold on as you find a seat. The bus may move before you sit down.
  - heading: 'Get off at your stop'
    icon: 'mdi:bell-ring-outline'
    picture: 'ride-stop'
    body:
      - >-
        Watch your map app, so you know when your stop is close. The driver calls out big stops too.
      - >-
        One block before your stop, press the yellow strip or the stop button. Get off at the back
        door if you can.
      - >-
        Need to change buses? A $2 fare is good for one ride. A 2-hour or 24-hour pass covers the
        change.
      - >-
        Missed your stop? Get off at the next one. Walk or roll back, or ride back from the stop
        across the street. You can always ask the driver.
    note: 'Routes and times change. Check RTC before you go.'
    links:
      - label: "RTC's first-timers' guide"
        href: 'https://www.rtcsnv.com/news/first-timers-guide-to-riding-rtc-transit/'
      - label: 'The Transit app at rtcsnv.com'
        href: 'https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/schedules-maps/transit-app/'
---
