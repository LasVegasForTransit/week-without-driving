---
title: 'Ride the bus for the first time'
order: 1
icon: 'mdi:bus-stop'
cardSummary: 'Find your bus, get on, and get off at your stop, in three steps.'
intro: >-
  Never taken the bus in Las Vegas, or not for a long time? RTC, the Regional Transportation
  Commission of Southern Nevada, runs the buses here. This guide walks you through one trip, from
  finding your bus to getting off at your stop.
steps:
  - heading: 'Find your bus and when it comes'
    icon: 'mdi:map-search-outline'
    body:
      - >-
        Type where you're going on [Plan a trip](/go). It opens bus directions in Google Maps, Apple
        Maps or the Transit app, with the route number, the stop to wait at and the time to be
        there.
      - >-
        RTC works with the free Transit app to show where each bus is right now. RTC's own rideRTC
        app shows arrival times too, and sells passes.
      - >-
        Note the time of the bus after yours as well, so missing one isn't a surprise. Have your
        fare ready: $2 for one ride, or $5 for 24 hours of rides. [Pay your bus
        fare](/guides/pay-your-fare) shows every way to pay.
  - heading: 'Find your bus at the stop and get on'
    icon: 'mdi:bus-side'
    body:
      - >-
        RTC buses stop only at bus stops. Many stops serve more than one route, so check the sign on
        the front of each bus for the route number and direction before you get on.
      - >-
        Stand where the driver can see you as the bus comes. Let people get off first, then get on
        at the front door.
      - >-
        Scan your pass, tap your card or phone, or pay with exact cash. Drivers don't carry change.
        Hold a handrail as you find a seat: the bus may start moving before you sit down.
  - heading: 'Ride, and get off at your stop'
    icon: 'mdi:bell-ring-outline'
    body:
      - >-
        Drivers announce major stops and transfer points. Following along on a map app shows you
        when your stop is close.
      - >-
        About a block before your stop, press the stop request button or the yellow strip. Get off
        at the back door when you can, so you don't block people getting on.
      - >-
        Changing buses? A $2 fare covers one ride; a 2-hour or 24-hour pass covers the change.
        Missed your stop? Get off at the next one and walk back, or catch the same route going the
        other way from the stop across the street. You can always ask the driver.
    note: 'Routes and times change. Check RTC before you travel.'
    links:

        href: 'https://www.rtcsnv.com/news/first-timers-guide-to-riding-rtc-transit/'
      - label: 'The Transit app at rtcsnv.com'
        href: 'https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/schedules-maps/transit-app/'
